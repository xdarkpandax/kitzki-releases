Kitzki privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Kitzki is a screenshot tool that runs on your computer. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. Your screenshots and clipboard history never leave your computer unless you share them yourself.

What stays on your computer

  • Screenshots. Kitzki captures the screen only when you press the capture hotkey or choose a capture from its menu. Captures go to your clipboard, or to the folder you choose.
  • Clipboard history. Kitzki keeps your recent copies (by default the last 500, for up to 30 days) so you can paste them again. The history is encrypted on disk with a key kept in your system's credential store (Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows). Copies that apps mark as private, such as passwords, are skipped, as are copies from password managers. You can clear the history, change the limits or turn it off in Settings.
  • Settings and logs. Your preferences and a diagnostic log are stored locally. The log records events such as the names of saved screenshots, never their content or what you copied. Kitzki never sends the log anywhere; you can send it to us yourself when reporting a problem.

Network use

Copies downloaded from this website check GitHub for new versions of Kitzki. That request includes nothing about you beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy policy. Copies installed from the Microsoft Store are updated by the Store and make no such check.

Permissions

On macOS Kitzki asks for Screen Recording, so it can capture the screen, and optionally Accessibility, so it can paste for you. On Windows it uses Windows Hello only to unlock older clipboard history; Kitzki never sees your PIN or biometric data.

Children

Kitzki does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/xdarkpandax/kitzki-releases.
